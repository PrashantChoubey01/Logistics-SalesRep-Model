📖 How It Works

1. Email Arrives

System receives an email from a customer, shipping partner, or sales person.

2. Identify & Route

AI determines who sent it and routes to the appropriate workflow path.

3. Process Information

Extracts shipment details, validates data, and enriches with port codes.

4. Generate Response

Creates appropriate reply: ask for missing info, confirm details, or acknowledge receipt.

5. Handle Partners

If from shipping partner, extracts rates and notifies sales team.

6. Save & Send

Saves conversation history and sends email to customer automatically.
